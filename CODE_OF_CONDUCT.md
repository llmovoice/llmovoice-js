# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project community.

Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims on evidence, respect privacy, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or personally abusive conduct. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community or project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's private contact channel. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest recused. This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

This concise policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant. Projects that require a formal enforcement process may adopt the full Contributor Covenant in a future release.
